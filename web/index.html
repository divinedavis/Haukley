FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Is Haukley really free?

Yes. Haukley is supported by ads, like broadcast TV, so there's no subscription and no credit card. You'll see a few short ad breaks during a film or episode.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start watching without signing up. Your place in each film is remembered on your device.

What can I watch on Haukley?

Classic films and series: Buster Keaton and Harold Lloyd comedies, Metropolis and Nosferatu, film noir, classic horror, swashbucklers, Pioneers of Black Cinema, NASA's Apollo mission films, early TV and modern animated shorts. Browse the full library.

How can these films be free?

Every title on Haukley is in the U.S. public domain or released by its makers under a Creative Commons license. We research the rights of each film before it goes up, and each film's page explains why it is free. Short ads pay for the streaming.

Where can I watch?

On the web at haukley.com/watch, on iPhone and Apple TV from the App Store, and on Amazon Fire TV. Roku, LG and Samsung TV apps are on the way. Haukley is available in the United States.

When is Haukley coming to my TV?

Leave your email and we'll send one note when Haukley is live on Roku, LG or Samsung, nothing else.

I made a film. Can it be on Haukley?

Yes — see For filmmakers. There's no fee, the deal is non-exclusive, and you earn half of the ad revenue your film generates.

How do I get help?

Email support@haukley.com or visit Support.
