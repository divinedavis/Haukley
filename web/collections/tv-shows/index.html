Classic TV & Docuseries

Series from the early days of television and from public agencies: NASA's own Apollo mission films, the 1954 Sherlock Holmes series, Betty White in Life with Elizabeth, the space serial Rocky Jones, Ub Iwerks's Flip the Frog cartoons, and Frank Capra's Why We Fight films made for the U.S. Army. Pick up any episode wherever you like.

Apollo: The Mission Films

Apollo: The Mission Films

1968 · Documentary · 9 episodes · NR

NASA's own films of the Apollo flights, from the first crewed test in Earth orbit to the last lunar landing.

Sherlock Holmes

Sherlock Holmes

1954 · Mystery · 8 episodes · NR

Ronald Howard plays a young, easygoing Sherlock Holmes solving Victorian London mysteries with Dr. Watson in this 1950s syndicated series.

Rocky Jones, Space Ranger

Rocky Jones, Space Ranger

1954 · Sci-Fi · 6 episodes · NR

Space Ranger Rocky Jones and the crew of the Orbit Jet patrol the United Worlds of the Solar System in this 1950s kids' space adventure.

Life with Elizabeth

Life with Elizabeth

1953 · Comedy · 7 episodes · NR

Betty White stars as a scatterbrained young wife whose everyday mishaps exasperate her husband in three short comic incidents per episode.

Flip the Frog

Flip the Frog

1930 · Animation · 6 episodes · NR

The first six cartoons from Ub Iwerks, co-creator of Mickey Mouse, starring his cheerful, rubbery frog in the early days of sound cartoons.

Caminandes

Caminandes

2013 · Animation · 3 episodes · NR

Koro, a stubborn Patagonian llama, schemes his way past fences, highways and one very pesky penguin in these dialogue-free cartoon shorts inspired by classic Chuck Jones gags.

Blender Open Shorts

Blender Open Shorts

2015 · Animation · 6 episodes · NR

A collection of award-winning animated shorts from Amsterdam's Blender Studio, from painterly space fables to slapstick rocket rides, each made entirely with free software.

Why We Fight

Why We Fight

1942 · Documentary · 8 episodes · NR

Frank Capra's seven-film U.S. Army series explaining the road to World War II, built from newsreels, captured footage and animated maps.

WWII Combat Reports

WWII Combat Reports

1942 · Documentary · 8 episodes · NR

Official combat documentaries shot by U.S. service cameramen, from Midway and Tarawa to Iwo Jima, including John Ford's and John Huston's war films.
