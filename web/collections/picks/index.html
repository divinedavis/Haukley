Haukley's Top 10 Picks

Our ten favorites from the library: the films we'd put on first for a friend who thinks old movies are slow. Fritz Lang's Metropolis and F. W. Murnau's Nosferatu still shape how science fiction and horror look a century later; Buster Keaton's The General and Harold Lloyd's Safety Last! are stunt comedy nobody has topped; and His Girl Friday talks faster than most films made since.

Metropolis

Metropolis

1927 · Sci-Fi · 2 hr 25 min · NR · Silent

In a towering future city split between idle rulers and underground workers, the ruler's son falls for a prophet of the workers, and an inventor builds a robot in her image.

The General

The General

1926 · Comedy · 1 hr 19 min · NR · Silent

When Union spies steal his beloved locomotive, a Southern train engineer chases it deep behind enemy lines in one of the great chase comedies.

Nosferatu

Nosferatu

1922 · Horror · 1 hr 3 min · NR · Silent

A real-estate clerk travels to the Carpathians to close a sale with the reclusive Count, who then follows him home bringing plague and death.

Night of the Living Dead

Night of the Living Dead

1968 · Horror · 1 hr 36 min · NR

Strangers barricade themselves inside a Pennsylvania farmhouse as the recently dead rise and close in through the night.

His Girl Friday

His Girl Friday

1940 · Comedy · 1 hr 32 min · NR

A fast-talking newspaper editor schemes to stop his star reporter, who is also his ex-wife, from quitting and remarrying by handing her one last big story.

Safety Last!

Safety Last!

1923 · Comedy · 1 hr 14 min · NR · Silent

A small-town go-getter promises his sweetheart he has made it big in the city, then has to climb the outside of a skyscraper to prove it.

Sherlock Jr.

Sherlock Jr.

1924 · Comedy · 46 min · NR · Silent

A small-town projectionist who dreams of being a detective falls asleep on the job and walks straight into the movie on screen.

Sintel

Sintel

2010 · Fantasy · 15 min · NR

A lone young woman crosses a harsh fantasy world to find the baby dragon she rescued and raised, which was snatched away from her.

The Phantom of the Opera

The Phantom of the Opera

1925 · Horror · 1 hr 32 min · NR · Silent

A disfigured genius haunting the cellars beneath the Paris Opera grooms a young singer for stardom, and will kill anyone who stands in his way.

Carnival of Souls

Carnival of Souls

1962 · Horror · 1 hr 18 min · NR

After surviving a car crash into a river, a church organist starts a new life in Utah but is stalked by a ghoulish figure and drawn to an abandoned pavilion.
