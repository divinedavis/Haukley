Landmarks of Cinema

Films that changed what movies could do: the first trip to the Moon on film, Nanook of the North, Man with a Movie Camera, the first Best Picture winner, and Salt of the Earth, made by blacklisted filmmakers in 1954. If you're starting a film education, start here.

Man with a Movie Camera

Man with a Movie Camera

1929 · Documentary · 1 hr 7 min · NR · Silent

A cameraman roams Soviet cities from dawn to dusk in a wordless, restlessly inventive portrait of modern life and of filmmaking itself.

All Quiet on the Western Front

All Quiet on the Western Front

1930 · War · 2 hr 13 min · NR

A class of German schoolboys enlists in the First World War full of patriotic fire and learns in the trenches what the war really costs.

A Trip to the Moon

A Trip to the Moon

1902 · Sci-Fi · 13 min · NR · Silent

A congress of astronomers fires itself to the moon in a giant shell, lands in the Man in the Moon's eye, and escapes the moon's strange inhabitants.

Metropolis

Metropolis

1927 · Sci-Fi · 2 hr 25 min · NR · Silent

In a towering future city split between idle rulers and underground workers, the ruler's son falls for a prophet of the workers, and an inventor builds a robot in her image.

Nosferatu

Nosferatu

1922 · Horror · 1 hr 3 min · NR · Silent

A real-estate clerk travels to the Carpathians to close a sale with the reclusive Count, who then follows him home bringing plague and death.

Wings

Wings

1927 · War · 2 hr 20 min · NR · Silent

Two small-town rivals for the same girl become fighter pilots and best friends in the skies over WWI France. It won the first Academy Award for Best Picture.

Sunrise

Sunrise

1927 · Romance · 1 hr 34 min · NR · Silent

A farmer tempted by a woman from the city plots to drown his wife, then spends a long, redemptive day in the city falling in love with her again.

The Crowd

The Crowd

1928 · Drama · 1 hr 33 min · NR · Silent

An ambitious young man arrives in New York sure he'll be somebody, then struggles through marriage, loss and a job as one more clerk in the crowd.

Nanook of the North

Nanook of the North

1922 · Documentary · 1 hr 17 min · NR · Silent

Robert Flaherty's landmark portrait of an Inuit family's year on the Hudson Bay coast: hunting, travel and building an igloo against the Arctic cold.

The Blue Angel

The Blue Angel

1930 · Drama · 1 hr 40 min · NR

A strict schoolteacher goes to a cabaret to catch his students and falls for its singer, Lola Lola, a love that costs him everything. Marlene Dietrich's breakthrough.

Salt of the Earth

Salt of the Earth

1954 · Drama · 1 hr 32 min · NR

When Mexican-American zinc miners in New Mexico strike and an injunction bars them from the picket line, their wives take their place.
