Classic Horror

Where screen horror comes from: German Expressionism's shadows, Lon Chaney's makeup, Bela Lugosi and Boris Karloff, and the low-budget independents of the 1950s and '60s, including Roger Corman and a young Francis Ford Coppola. Night of the Living Dead fell into the public domain on release because its prints went out without a copyright notice, which is why it is free here.
