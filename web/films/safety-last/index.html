1923 · Comedy · 1 hr 14 min · NR · Silent

One man. Twelve stories. No net.

A small-town go-getter promises his sweetheart he has made it big in the city, then has to climb the outside of a skyscraper to prove it.

Directed by Fred C. Newmeyer · Sam Taylor · Starring Harold Lloyd · Mildred Davis

Why this film is free to watch

It was published in 1923. In the United States, films published in 1930 or earlier are now in the public domain, so they belong to everyone.

It is a silent film: the story is told in pictures and title cards. Haukley plays it as it was released, so some copies have a music track and some play in silence.
