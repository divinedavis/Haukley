Go West

1925 · Comedy · 1 hr 8 min · NR · Silent

A drifter, a cow and a thousand steers.

A drifter with no luck heads west, takes a ranch job and befriends a cow named Brown Eyes, then has to herd a thousand steers through downtown Los Angeles.

Directed by Buster Keaton · Starring Buster Keaton · Howard Truesdale · Kathleen Myers

Why this film is free to watch

It was published in 1925. In the United States, films published in 1930 or earlier are now in the public domain, so they belong to everyone.

It is a silent film: the story is told in pictures and title cards. Haukley plays it as it was released, so some copies have a music track and some play in silence.
