Our Hospitality

1923 · Comedy · 1 hr 14 min · NR · Silent

A feud, a family dinner, and a very long waterfall.

A young man travels South to claim his inheritance and falls for a girl whose family has sworn a blood feud against his, and they cannot shoot him while he is a guest in their house.

Directed by Buster Keaton · John G. Blystone · Starring Buster Keaton · Natalie Talmadge · Joe Roberts

Why this film is free to watch
