Let There Be Light

1946 · Documentary · 58 min · NR

John Huston's long-suppressed film on war trauma.

John Huston follows soldiers treated for psychological trauma at an Army hospital after World War II, a film the Army withheld from the public until 1980.

Directed by John Huston · Narrated by Walter Huston · Music by Dimitri Tiomkin · Produced by the U.S. Army Signal Corps

Watch free

Content note: Real psychiatric patients describing wartime trauma.

Why this film is free to watch
