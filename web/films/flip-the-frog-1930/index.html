Flip the Frog

1930 · Animation · 6 episodes · NR

Ub Iwerks' first star, from 1930.

The first six cartoons from Ub Iwerks, co-creator of Mickey Mouse, starring his cheerful, rubbery frog in the early days of sound cartoons.

Produced and drawn by Ub Iwerks · Celebrity Productions (Pat Powers) · Released by Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer

Watch free

Episodes

  1. Fiddlesticks · 6 min

    Flip dances and plays piano with a violin-playing mouse at a woodland concert in the first Flip cartoon.

  2. Flying Fists · 7 min

    Flip takes on a much bigger opponent in a knockabout boxing match.

  3. The Village Barber · 7 min

    Flip's barbershop turns into chaos when his customers get more than a trim.

  4. Little Orphan Willie · 6 min

    A stork leaves a mischievous orphan on Flip's doorstep on a snowy night.

  5. The Cuckoo Murder Case · 8 min

    A stormy night and a spooky house send Flip on a ghoulish murder mystery.

  6. Puddle Pranks · 6 min

    Flip woos a sweetheart frog in and around the pond.
