Blender Open Shorts

2015 · Animation · 6 episodes · NR

Animated shorts from Blender Studio.

A collection of award-winning animated shorts from Amsterdam's Blender Studio, from painterly space fables to slapstick rocket rides, each made entirely with free software.

Produced by Blender Studio (Blender Institute / Blender Foundation) · Directed by Beorn Leonard · Hjalti Hjalmarsson · Daniel Martinez Lara · Rik Schutte · Andy Goralczyk

Watch free

Episodes

  1. Glass Half · 3 min

    Two amateur art critics bicker their way through a gallery, causing chaos for everyone else, until they finally find a piece they both love.

  2. The Daily Dweebs · 1 min

    In 1950s American suburbia, a mischievous pet named Dixey has a very bad day with a backyard chicken.

  3. Hero · 4 min

    Rendered as a living sketchbook, a soldier and a mysterious rival clash in a dazzling 2D-in-3D action showdown.

  4. Coffee Run · 3 min

    Fueled by caffeine, a young woman sprints through the bittersweet memories of a past relationship.

  5. WING IT! · 4 min

    An uptight engineer's day goes sideways when an overeager wannabe pilot launches them both skyward in an out-of-control space shuttle.

  6. Singularity · 7 min

    When a cosmic event swallows his icy home, a small space critter seeks refuge among luminous creatures watched over by a mysterious celestial being.
