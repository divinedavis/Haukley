A Trip to the Moon

1902 · Sci-Fi · 13 min · NR · Silent

The first science-fiction film ever made.

A congress of astronomers fires itself to the moon in a giant shell, lands in the Man in the Moon's eye, and escapes the moon's strange inhabitants.

Directed by Georges Méliès · Starring Georges Méliès · Bleuette Bernon

Watch free

Why this film is free to watch

It was published in 1902. In the United States, films published in 1930 or earlier are now in the public domain, so they belong to everyone.

It is a silent film: the story is told in pictures and title cards. Haukley plays it as it was released, so some copies have a music track and some play in silence.
