The Crowd

1928 · Drama · 1 hr 33 min · NR · Silent

One man among millions.

An ambitious young man arrives in New York sure he'll be somebody, then struggles through marriage, loss and a job as one more clerk in the crowd.

Directed by King Vidor · Starring Eleanor Boardman · James Murray · Bert Roach

Watch free

Why this film is free to watch

It was published in 1928. In the United States, films published in 1930 or earlier are now in the public domain, so they belong to everyone.

It is a silent film: the story is told in pictures and title cards. Haukley plays it as it was released, so some copies have a music track and some play in silence.
