Privacy Policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

Haukley ("Haukley", "we", "us") runs the haukley.com website and the Haukley apps for Roku, Amazon Fire TV, Google TV / Android TV, Apple TV and iPhone (together, the "Service"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Haukley is free and ad-supported; you can watch without creating an account.

Advertising

Ads in the Haukley apps are served by third-party ad technology, including Google's Interactive Media Ads SDK on iPhone, Apple TV, Fire TV and Google TV, and the Roku Advertising Framework on Roku. These partners may collect device information such as an advertising identifier, IP address, and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads. Their use of that information is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Roku: Roku Privacy Policy

You can limit ad personalization in your device settings — for example "Limit ad tracking" on Roku, "Opt out of interest-based ads" on Fire TV and Android, and the Tracking setting on iPhone and Apple TV.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your email address or share it with advertisers.
  • We don't require an account or collect payment information.
  • We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email privacy@haukley.com and we'll delete it.

How long we keep it

Launch-list emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Film submissions are kept while we review them and, for films we don't carry, deleted within 12 months. Ad events are kept as long as needed to calculate and audit filmmaker payments. Watch progress stays on your device until you delete the app or clear its data.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe from any email we send, or ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, by emailing privacy@haukley.com. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have additional rights; we'll honor them for everyone.

Security

We use HTTPS, restrict database access so the public apps can only add records (never read them), and limit who can access stored data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and for material changes we'll give notice on haukley.com.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@haukley.com.
